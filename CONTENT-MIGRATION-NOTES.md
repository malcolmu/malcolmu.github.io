# Content migration notes — local prototype

Last updated: 28 July 2026. This is a local design build, not a content-approved replacement site.

## Structural changes made

- **Visit and Accessibility merged into `/visit`.** Access information is now part of the main planning journey, rather than a separate page. It retains a visible note to confirm site-specific details.
- **Volunteering, Volunteer and support material merged into `/get-involved`.** This removes the duplicate volunteer routes and gives support, donation and volunteering one clearer home.
- **Wedding FAQs moved into `/weddings`.** Weddings and `/venue-hire` remain separate, because their audiences, enquiries and practical information are different.
- **Blog is now clearly exposed at `/blog`.** The 14 visible current blog entries have individual `/blog/[slug]` routes; the earlier `/news` prototype routes redirect to them.
- **Heritage projects were rolled into `/our-story`.** “Above us, only sky?” and “20th-century chapel” now appear as sections on the main Story page. Their earlier prototype routes redirect to the matching sections.
- **What’s On remains calendar-first.** Current-site-based July/August records have internal detail pages; Eventbrite remains the intended booking destination.

## Copy edits and omissions

- Rewrote page introductions in a concise, consistent voice for the approved “Open to the Sky” direction. These are working copy, not final approved editorial copy.
- Replaced dated 2024 headings and generic dated calls-to-action with evergreen wording.
- Did not import old form submissions, contacts, Wix members, comments or spam. The future CMS/content migration should select only approved public content.
- Did not reproduce the hundreds of historic event listings. The prototype includes a focused current/upcoming set: Sound Bath (29 July and 2 August 2026), Twelfth Night (2 August), an Open Day, Makers Market (16 August) and Savera UK’s Culture at the Bombed Out Church (29 August).
- The dates and times displayed for the two Sound Baths, Twelfth Night and Savera UK follow the current What’s On listings audited on 28 July 2026. Event descriptions, prices, access details and direct Eventbrite links still need confirmation and are marked as time-sensitive.
- All 14 visible blog headlines and publication dates have been retained in the local news archive. Their body copy has been condensed and lightly rewritten into article-specific local summaries; export and approve the original Wix articles, captions, author bylines and featured images before launch.
- Garden Bar now includes an Opening Times section explaining that it is open for the summer season, weather-dependent and updated weekly through `@bocgardenbar`. Exact weekly hours and seasonal offers still require current confirmation.
- Current wedding capacity, pricing, suppliers, licence/legal details and wet-weather arrangements are not asserted; the page directs users to an enquiry flow.
- Current jobs are intentionally not invented. `/jobs` is an evergreen vacancy route that should carry only live vacancies.
